A friendly family money app, wrapped in a cosy village.

Every family member is a house. Data screens lead with plain answers (“You’re up RM 1,240 this year”) before any chart. Built as an iOS home-screen web app — portrait only, three languages, light & dark. Every screen below is the real app running live, not a mock-up.

Principles

Designed for finance-shy parents, brand-new investors and grandparents who just want to see how the family is doing.

Design system

Colour

Warm paper and white cards keep data calm. One leaf-green accent, sunflower for money coming in, wood for the village chrome. Gain/loss never relies on colour alone — every amount carries ▲/▼ and words for screen readers, and people can switch to China-style “red means up”.

Type

A chunky, rounded display face paired with a crisp UI face — each with a partner for Simplified Chinese and Thai. Sizes are in rem and follow iOS Dynamic Type (-apple-system-body) plus an in-app A / A+ / A++ setting. Thai gets extra line height for stacked tone marks; numbers are always tabular.

Components

Icons

One 24px line family, 2px rounded strokes. The Family tab keeps a little full-colour village as the signature mark.

Village kit

All art is generated SVG — no image files — so every house is unique to its owner and the whole village re-lights for night mode. Houses grow with the owner’s total (levels 1–5); privacy-hidden totals show a neutral house.

Screens

Live iframes of the app in “shot” mode (demo data, nothing saved). Tap around inside any phone — they are fully interactive.

Three languages, one layout

Switching is instant (no reload) and remembered per person. Tab labels, buttons and house tags auto-fit instead of truncating; Chinese uses compact 万 formatting, Thai uses the Buddhist calendar and baht. Family-typed names and goals are never auto-translated.
